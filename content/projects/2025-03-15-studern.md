## Overview

**StudErn** (Student + Earn) is a campus micro-employment platform that connects students and job seekers with recruiters and companies through paid short-term tasks, internships and projects. Its core idea is simple: **finished work should become checkable evidence for employers**, not just another claim on a CV.

StudErn is my team's Final Year Design Project (FYDP) at **United International University**, built under the title *"Design and Implementation of a Socio-technical Employment Platform to Bridge the Academia-Industry Practice Gap"*. It now runs online as a pilot.

Live: [https://studern.2haas.com](https://studern.2haas.com/)

---

## The Problem

Many graduates enter the job market with no work experience, and academic learning does not match industry practice well. The gap is not only technical. It also involves skills, expectations, trust and coordination between students, academia and industry.

We studied this gap through focus groups and structured interviews with students, academic professionals and industry practitioners, plus an online survey of **84 respondents**:

- **76%** named lack of real-world experience as a key challenge.
- **Fake or exaggerated skills** were the top reason employers lose trust in applicants.
- **49%** said they were missing career guidance.

The usual signals, a degree name or a CGPA, don't tell an employer what a student can actually do. A student without a track record can't get the first job that would give them one.

### Objectives

1. Give students paid, short, real work that looks like industry practice.
2. Turn every finished job into evidence the next employer can open and verify.
3. Make matching transparent, with no hidden scores or proxy signals like CGPA.
4. Give recruiters and admins a safe, precise tool to run hiring and moderation at scale.

---

## How It Works

1. **Find work you can actually get:** openings written for people without a track record. Every posting shows the pay, the closing date and how many places are open.
2. **Apply without a CV you don't have:** the StudErn profile *is* the application. Education, skills, projects, achievements and work, entered once.
3. **Do the work, keep the proof:** short projects, internships and paid tasks with a real employer on the other end.
4. **Carry the record forward:** every hire adds a work record linked to the original posting, and a public link lets any employer check it.

## Features

### For Students and Job Seekers

1. **Evidence profile:** a structured profile of education, skills, projects, achievements and work experience.
2. **Transparent match score:** every opening gets a score out of 100, with plain-language reasons factor by factor, plus suggestions for in-demand skills.
3. **Job browser and saved jobs:** search, filter and bookmark openings.
4. **Application tracking:** see each application's stage, upcoming interviews and outcomes in one dashboard.
5. **CV builder:** works without an account; the browser keeps the draft locally and the server renders an ATS-friendly single-column PDF without storing anything. After sign-up, the draft can be imported into the profile.
6. **CV verification link:** a public link that lets any employer verify the student's work record.
7. **Mock tests:** book live practice interviews or tests with providers selected by the StudErn team, and get plain feedback on what to work on.

### For Recruiters and Companies

1. **Company verification:** companies must be verified before they can post.
2. **Team management:** multiple recruiters per company, with company-level memberships.
3. **Six-stage hiring pipeline:** move applicants through *Applied → Screening → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer → Hired* (or reject), schedule interviews and keep private notes.
4. **Auto-closing postings:** a posting closes itself once every place is filled.
5. **Paid posting packages:** the first posting is free; further packages are paid through **bKash**, with payment locking to prevent double processing.
6. **Shareable job links:** server-rendered share pages with Open Graph images for clean previews on social and chat apps.

### For Platform Admins

1. **Live metrics:** platform activity, the selection funnel and user demographics.
2. **Moderation:** suspend postings, hold or ban companies, and ban or suppress accounts.
3. **Verification review:** approve or reject company verification documents.
4. **Billing and partners:** manage packages, discounts and partner companies.
5. **Privilege-based access:** fine-grained admin privileges instead of all-or-nothing admin roles.

## Security and Trust

- **Device-bound JWT authentication**, with social sign-in through **Google, LinkedIn and GitHub**.
- **OTP-based contact verification** for email and phone, with per-contact and per-IP rate limits.
- **Role-based access control**, covered by negative tests that check one role can't reach another's data.
- **Redis-backed rate limiting and caching** to keep the pilot reliable on tight resources.
- **Account lifecycle controls:** restriction, reactivation and a data-deletion flow with a published policy.

## Architecture

StudErn uses a classic client-server design. The frontend compiles to plain HTML and JavaScript and is served from Cloudflare Pages with no server code of its own. A single small Pages Function sends social-media and chat bots to a server-rendered share page, so shared job links get a proper title and image.

The backend is a single multi-stage Docker image that runs the API server and the queue worker side by side. Database migrations run only behind an explicit flag, so a plain container restart can never change the schema by accident.

## Design

The interface follows a design system we call **"The Quiet Ledger"**: one restrained brand teal (`#057479`), cool blue-grey neutrals, Archivo for display type and Public Sans for body text. Surfaces are flat and separated by hairline rules rather than shadows. The goal was for every screen, from a job card to a privilege-revocation dialog, to look *correct before it looks attractive*, because the platform handles personal data, job applications and account privileges.

It meets the WCAG AA baseline: AA contrast in light and dark themes, full keyboard navigation and support for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Development Process

We built StudErn in **four Agile iterations** using a human-centred, co-design approach. Requirements came straight from the stakeholder research, and each design decision maps back to a finding (for example, missing career guidance led to the explained match score and skill suggestions). We evaluated it through prototype walkthroughs, structured interviews and system-log analysis.
