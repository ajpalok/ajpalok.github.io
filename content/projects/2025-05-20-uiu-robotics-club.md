## Overview

UIU Robotics is a student-run club of undergraduate and graduate students from United International University. The purpose of the Organization is to explore new engineering technology, learn crucial skills and promote the field of robotics.

### Objectives:

We aim to provide a platform for students to learn and collaborate on robotics projects, showcase the projects and activities of the club, foster a community of robotics enthusiasts within the university, and encourage students to participate in robotics competitions and events.

 1. Showcase the projects and activities of the club.
 2. Provide a platform for students to learn and collaborate on robotics projects.
 3. Foster a community of robotics enthusiasts within the university.
 4. Encourage students to participate in robotics competitions and events.

## Conclusion

The UIU Robotics Club website serves as a valuable resource for students interested in robotics, providing them with the tools and information they need to succeed in their projects and competitions.
