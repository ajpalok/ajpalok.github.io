## Overview

Perfex is a powerful open-source CRM that helps businesses manage their customer relationships and sales processes. It provides a range of features including lead management, sales tracking, and customer support.
Perfex is designed to be user-friendly and customizable, making it suitable for businesses of all sizes. It offers a range of integrations with other tools and services, allowing businesses to streamline their workflows and improve efficiency.

---

### Tech Stack

- **Frontend**:
    - HTML, CSS, JavaScript
    - Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: 
    - PHP
    - CodeIgniter
    - MySQL
    - Composer
    - Cron Jobs
- **Hosting**: 
    - cPanel (Shared Production Server)
    - XAMPP (Local Development Server)
